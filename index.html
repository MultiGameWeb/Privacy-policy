AI Chess is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information may be collected, why it is used, how it is protected, and how users can request account and data deletion.

Policy details: AI Chess may use account, gameplay and security-related information to provide cloud features, player profiles, multiplayer functionality, statistics and other app functionality.

This Privacy Policy applies to the AI Chess application and related services operated by VK APP Studio.

  • Wins, losses and draws
  • Puzzle completion history

2.3 Virtual Currency

  • In-game coin balance
  • Daily puzzle rewards

  • Other eligible in-game rewards

Limited device-related information may be processed where technically necessary for application security, fraud prevention, referral abuse prevention, compatibility or service reliability.

User Rights

Account Deletion

Users may request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

In-app deletion: Profile → Settings → Delete Account → Confirm Deletion

1. Self-Service In-App Deletion

When available, users can delete their account directly from the app using the account deletion option in Settings.

2. Web / Email Request

Users who have uninstalled the app can submit an account deletion request by email.

Email: kammarivinaykumar981@gmail.com

Subject: Account Deletion Request

Include the registered email address or other information needed to identify the account.

3. Data That May Be Deleted

  • Firebase Authentication account
  • User profile information
  • Username and avatar/profile information
  • Rating and related statistics
  • Referral information and codes
  • Stored game-related records associated with the account
  • Chat records where applicable

4. Deletion Time

Deletion requests are processed within 7 business days, subject to applicable technical, legal, security or fraud-prevention retention requirements.

Important: Some records may need to be retained where required by law, security, fraud prevention, dispute handling or other legitimate obligations.

Technical Updates

Latest App Improvements

Recent technical and visual improvements made to AI Chess.

♟️

Front Pawn Uniform Design Update

Previously, front pawns could display different visual variants depending on their board column. The pawn design has been standardized.

  • All 8 White front pawns use the same classic Staunton-inspired look.
  • All 8 Black front pawns use the same classic Staunton-inspired look.
  • Smooth pedestal base, collar ring, spherical head and subtle gloss.
  • Consistent pawn appearance across every file/column.

Other pieces retain their distinct traditional shapes:

  • Rook / Elephant
  • King
  • Bishop / Camel
  • Knight / Horse
  • Queen
🛡️

App Force Stopping / Auto-Closing Resolved

1. Android Manifest Service Merge Fix

Removed a problematic service override associated with startup package-manager issues and used standard metadata configuration where appropriate.

2. Firebase Singleton Initialization Protection

Firebase-dependent managers could previously throw an IllegalStateException during early startup. Startup handling was changed to use null-safe initialization methods.

3. Fail-Safe Startup

  • UserProfileManager initialization is protected.
  • ReferralManager initialization is protected.
  • Deep-link parsing is protected against startup exceptions.
  • Safer Firebase option fallback handling was added where required.
✓ Startup stability improved
Known startup failure paths were addressed.
